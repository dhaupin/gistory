# Why Gistory

Good prompts are reusable assets, and today they are written once and lost.

## Scattered across chats and notes

A chat window forgets nothing but finds nothing: scrolling back through months
of messages is not a library. Notes apps store the text but know nothing about
`{{variables}}`, so every reuse is a manual find-and-replace. Neither is built
for the moment you actually want the prompt.

## Reuse means retyping

A prompt with placeholders is a template, and a template should fill in.
Without that, reuse costs a copy, a paste, and a careful pass over every
placeholder, which is exactly the friction that stops people reusing the good
prompt and makes them write a worse one from scratch.

## Sync should not cost your privacy

The obvious fix, a hosted prompt library, asks you to hand your prompts to
someone else's server. Gistory keeps them in your browser instead, and when you
want them on more than one device it replicates ciphertext the relay cannot
read: no account, no plaintext, no third party holding the text you think in.
