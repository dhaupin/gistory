# What Gistory is

A personal prompt manager and AI prompt library, running in your browser.

Gistory is a browser-based tool for writing, organizing, and reusing text
prompts. It runs entirely on your device: your library lives in your browser,
and optional sync moves it between your own devices as ciphertext. No account
is required to use any of it.

## The library

Every prompt is a saved entry with a title, body, tags, and an optional
project. Write one once, and it is there to find in a keystroke, copy, or fork
into a variant instead of starting over.

## The templates

Prompts carry `{{variable}}` placeholders. Copying a prompt opens a fill-in
form, and Gistory copies the text with every variable substituted, so a
reusable template becomes a finished prompt in one step. Global and per-thread
variable pools keep the common values close.

## The organization

Tags, projects, drag ordering, pinned threads, and forked variants keep a
growing library navigable. Search covers everything, and usage tracking
surfaces the prompts you actually reach for.

## The sync

Multi-device sync is end-to-end encrypted. Devices pair with a passphrase and a
pairing code instead of an account; prompts are encrypted on your device before
upload, so the relay stores only blobs it cannot decrypt. There is no reset and
no recovery: the passphrase never leaves your devices, which is the point.

## What it is not

Not a hosted service that holds your text, and not an analytics product. There
are no accounts, no tracking, and no third party in the loop when you copy a
prompt.
